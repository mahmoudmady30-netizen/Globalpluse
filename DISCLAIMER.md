# Disclaimer

This project displays news headlines and automated sentiment scores for informational and educational purposes only.

- It is **not** investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell any security, or a signal of any kind.
- Sentiment scores are generated by a third-party automated system (Marketaux) and can be wrong, delayed, or missing context.
- News being "trending" does not mean a price move hasn't already happened — acting on a headline after it's widely circulated is one of the riskiest ways to trade.
- Nothing in this app should be treated as a guarantee of future performance. Markets involve real risk of loss.
- Always verify information against official sources (the exchange itself, the company's investor relations page, or a licensed financial advisor) before making any financial decision.
